Our Legacy

From a single naturopathy center in Durg to a foundation carrying that work into the wider community — this is the story of Shree Sahaj Nature Cure Foundation, sixteen years in the making.

2010

A Center Is Founded

Dr. Nirmala Gupta opens Shree Sahaj Naturopathy & Yoga Center in Durg, Chhattisgarh, with a simple purpose — to serve people through nature's own methods of healing.

2011 – 2020

Growing Roots

Over the next decade, the center's offerings grow — hydrotherapy, mud therapy, yoga sessions, acupressure, and physiotherapy — alongside community participation in International Yoga Day, National Naturopathy Day, and local health campaigns.

Ongoing

Reaching the Community

Women's Day celebrations, blood donation drives, and awareness campaigns become a regular part of our work — carrying the belief that wellness is something to be shared, not kept within four walls.

2026

Shree Sahaj Nature Cure Foundation Is Born

Sixteen years after that first center opened its doors, the mission is formally incorporated as Shree Sahaj Nature Cure Foundation — a Section 8 company registered to carry the work of natural healing further into the community, education, and outreach, while our original clinic, Shree Sahaj Naturopathy & Yoga Center (MSME Registered), continues its everyday practice.

Today

Two Units, One Mission

Today, the Foundation and the clinic work side by side — one delivering day-to-day naturopathy, yoga, and wellness services at nominal charges, the other carrying that same spirit into health camps, awareness programs, and the wider community.

“Every year, every camp, every session has added a thread to a story that began with one simple intention — to heal, naturally.”
